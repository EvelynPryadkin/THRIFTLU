# LU Thrift Requirements

## Problem

Students cannot see what is available in the Lawrence University thrift
room without visiting and searching through the room physically.

## Project Goal

LU Thrift will allow students to browse available thrift-room items online
and reserve an item for pickup.

## Required Features

- Students can create an account and sign in.
- Students can browse available items.
- Students can search and filter items.
- Students can reserve an available item.
- Students can view and cancel their reservations.
- Staff can create, edit, publish, and archive items.
- Staff can confirm when an item is collected.
- The system prevents two students from reserving the same item.

## User Roles

### Student

- Browse available items
- Reserve an item
- Cancel their own reservation
- View their reservation history

### Staff

- Manage thrift-room inventory
- View active reservations
- Confirm item pickup
- Mark an item missing or unavailable

### Administrator

- Assign staff access
- Change reservation settings
- Review important system actions

## Outside the Initial Scope

- Student-to-student exchanges
- Payments
- Ratings
- Real-time chat
- Waitlists
- Text-message notifications
